Reasons for Using Website Terms and Conditions

Website Terms and Conditions are important for protecting both the website owner and users. They act as a legal agreement that outlines the rules, responsibilities, and limitations involved when someone uses your site. For a platform like Donor Connect, Terms and Conditions ensure that users understand how to properly interact with the services offered — such as filling out donation forms, attending camps, or accessing information. These terms help prevent misuse, such as false registrations or inappropriate behavior. They also limit the site’s legal liability in case of technical errors, delays, or incorrect information. In addition, the terms often include a privacy section, which explains how personal data (like name, contact info, and blood group) is collected and used. Overall, Terms and Conditions help build trust, provide legal protection, and create a clear understanding between the user and the platform — making the experience safer and more professional for everyone.

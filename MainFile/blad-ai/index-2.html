Popular Campaigns

Blood Group Collection

We collect and organize donor information based on blood groups to ensure quick and accurate matching during emergencies. Whether you’re A, B, AB, or O—positive or negative—every type matters. This system helps connect recipients with compatible donors faster, saving crucial time when every second counts.

Free Group Checking

We offer free blood group checking to help individuals know their type and encourage informed, life-saving blood donations.

Blood Donation Camp

Donor Connect hosts safe blood donation camps to raise awareness, collect blood easily, and encourage communities to save lives together.
